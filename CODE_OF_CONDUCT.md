# Code of Conduct

DevRelay is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, specific, and considerate. Respect differing viewpoints,
focus criticism on the work, protect private information, and accept reasonable
maintainer direction about scope and conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, sustained personal attacks, or publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, and
other participation that violates this policy. Responses may range from a
private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, based on severity and pattern.

Report security vulnerabilities through `SECURITY.md`, not through this
process. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner at
`garrett.audet@gmail.com`. Do not disclose
sensitive report details in a public issue.

Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest. A maintainer named in a report
must not make the final enforcement decision when another qualified maintainer
is available. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical while
allowing a fair investigation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
