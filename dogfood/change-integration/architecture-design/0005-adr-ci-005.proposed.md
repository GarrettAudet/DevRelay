# Keep repository integration distinct from SystemVerification and BusinessAcceptance

## Status

Proposed

## Context and problem statement

DevRelay needs replaceable verifier capabilities without transferring subject binding, obligation expansion, evidence validation, policy, Gate, integration, traceability, or promotion authority to verifier adapters.

## Decision drivers

- Determinism
- Exact-subject isolation
- Provider neutrality
- Least authority
- Traceability
- Verifiability

## Considered options

- OPT-CI-INTEGRATED-NOT-ACCEPTED
- OPT-CI-INTEGRATE-AND-ACCEPT

## Decision outcome

Chosen: OPT-CI-INTEGRATED-NOT-ACCEPTED. The selected boundary preserves canonical DevRelay authority while keeping concrete verifiers replaceable, evidence-only, bounded, and auditable.

## Consequences

- Positive: test runners, analyzers, scanners, humans, and future verifiers can implement the same evidence-producing port.
- Negative: every verifier binding requires version-pinned configuration, declared authority, host enforcement, independence evaluation when required, and conformance evidence before invocation.
