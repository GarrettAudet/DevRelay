# Ship JSON Schema 2020-12 first behind a contract-kind port

## Status

Proposed

## Context and problem statement

DevRelay needs replaceable contract generation without transferring routing, validation, compatibility, traceability, or promotion authority to generator adapters.

## Decision drivers

- Determinism
- Compatibility
- Provider neutrality
- Traceability
- Verifiability

## Considered options

- OPT-CG-JSON-SCHEMA-FIRST-PORTABLE
- OPT-CG-MULTIFORMAT-SPECIAL-CASES

## Decision outcome

Chosen: OPT-CG-JSON-SCHEMA-FIRST-PORTABLE. The selected boundary preserves canonical DevRelay authority while keeping generated contracts independently validated and auditable.

## Consequences

- Positive: contract formats and best-in-class generator tools remain replaceable behind one semantic port.
- Negative: each contract kind requires a pinned validator, compatibility policy, and conformance evidence before WorkBreakdown.
