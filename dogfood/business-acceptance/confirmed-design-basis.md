# BusinessAcceptanceGate confirmed design basis

1. **D-BA-BOUNDARY-001**

   BusinessAcceptanceGate is Core-owned authority with no adapter execution; external evidence collectors may be introduced later only as bounded upstream producers.

2. **D-BA-SUBJECT-001**

   The acceptance subject binds the exact verified SystemVerificationResult, paired RequirementsBaseline and ProjectOverviewBaseline, ArchitectureBaseline, ContractDisposition, repository release snapshot, policy, business evidence, and TraceabilityGraph checkpoint.

3. **D-BA-COVERAGE-001**

   Core exhaustively disposes every approved business objective and success metric as satisfied, failed, or needs-evidence and rejects omitted, duplicate, or unapproved scope.

4. **D-BA-APPROVAL-001**

   Evidence evaluation produces a candidate only; a separate exact owner approval over raw candidate bytes produces the authoritative BusinessAcceptanceRecord.

5. **D-BA-OUTCOMES-001**

   Closed outcomes are accepted, rejected, needs-evidence, baseline-drift, and unable-to-proceed; only accepted completes the V1 construction lifecycle.

6. **D-BA-TRACE-001**

   Traceability vocabulary 1.5 adds an acceptance horizon and business-acceptance-record node, with forward objective/metric accepted-by and verified-by facts and no inverse edges.

7. **D-BA-NO-DEPLOY-001**

   Business acceptance records product acceptance only; release, deployment, production verification, monitoring, and operations remain outside V1.
