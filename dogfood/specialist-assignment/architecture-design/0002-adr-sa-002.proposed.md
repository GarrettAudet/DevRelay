# Separate generation from validation, diff, and Gate authority

## Status

Proposed

## Context and problem statement

DevRelay needs replaceable assignment generation without transferring routing, validation, assignment-policy, traceability, or promotion authority to ranker adapters.

## Decision drivers

- Determinism
- Assignment policy
- Provider neutrality
- Traceability
- Verifiability

## Considered options

- OPT-SA-SEPARATED-TRUST-BOUNDARIES
- OPT-SA-RANKER-OWNS-ELIGIBILITY

## Decision outcome

Chosen: OPT-SA-SEPARATED-TRUST-BOUNDARIES. The selected boundary preserves canonical DevRelay authority while keeping generated assignments independently validated and auditable.

## Consequences

- Positive: assignment formats and best-in-class ranker tools remain replaceable behind one semantic port.
- Negative: each profile capability requires a pinned validator, assignment-policy policy, and conformance evidence before WorkExecution.
