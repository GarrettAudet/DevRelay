# DevRelay V1 lifecycle approval

The product owner approved establishment of one project-wide DevRelay ProjectOverview baseline and confirmed that V1 contains exactly these lifecycle components in order:

1. RequirementsGathering (module)
2. RequirementsGate (gate)
3. ArchitectureDiscovery (module, conditional)
4. ArchitectureDesign (module)
5. ArchitectureGate (gate)
6. ContractGeneration (module, conditional)
7. WorkBreakdown (module)
8. WorkDependencyAnalysis (module)
9. SpecialistAssignment (module)
10. WorkExecution (module)
11. WorkItemVerification (module)
12. ChangeIntegration (module)
13. SystemVerification (module)
14. BusinessAcceptance (module)

ArchitectureDiscovery applies to an existing repository without a baseline. ContractGeneration applies when APIs, schemas, events, protocols, or other formal contracts are required. TraceabilityGraph remains a cross-cutting infrastructure service and is not an additional lifecycle stage.
